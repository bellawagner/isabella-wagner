      My Design Journey

      Netherlands
      Born in Almere, a city that didn't exist until someone decided to build it. Pulled from the sea, planned from zero, nothing there by accident. I didn't grow up thinking about design theory, but looking back, growing up somewhere so deliberately made probably had more influence than I realise. It is hard not to develop an instinct for intention when the ground you are standing on was once water.
      China
      Raised in Shanghai, which means I grew up thinking contradiction was normal. Old and new, east and west, calligraphy class in the morning and internet culture by afternoon. Nobody there treats tradition and modernity like they are in conflict, they just both exist, loudly, at the same time. That probably explains why I have never found it hard to work across very different markets or cultures. It just feels like home.
      Germany
      Frankfurt is where I figured out what I actually wanted to do with design. It is not the most obvious creative city but that is almost the point, here design has to justify itself. It has to work. Being here pushed me toward systems thinking, toward caring about handoffs and workflows as much as aesthetics. You cannot get away with something just looking good. It has to hold up.
